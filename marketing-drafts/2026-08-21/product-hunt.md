## Product name

Fast Image Compression

## Tagline

Compress images locally in your browser

## Description (strict-field version)

Compress and resize images in your browser without uploading image files to the application's server. Adjust quality, dimensions, format, or a target size; preview the result; and download individual or batch files. Free, with no registration.

## Maker first comment

Hi Product Hunt — I built Fast Image Compression to make a common image-preparation step quicker and more private.

The tool processes selected images locally in the browser instead of uploading the image files to the application's server. It supports single and multi-image workflows, quality and dimension controls, target-size workflows, browser-supported output formats, previews, and downloads. It is free to use and does not require registration.

The main use cases I had in mind were preparing images for websites, WordPress, email, and forms with file-size limits. I have also added practical guides for people who want help choosing formats or quality settings.

I would especially value feedback on:

1. whether the controls are understandable on the first try;
2. whether the target-size workflow is useful;
3. output quality across different types of images and browsers.

Thanks for trying it.
