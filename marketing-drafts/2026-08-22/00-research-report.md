## Product facts rechecked

The live homepage and repository README were checked on 2026-08-22. Safe marketing claims remain:

- Fast Image Compression is free to use without registration.
- Selected image files are processed locally in the browser rather than uploaded to the application's server.
- The tool supports single- and multi-image workflows, quality and dimension controls, target-size workflows, browser-supported output formats, previews, and downloads.
- Do not claim that the whole website is tracking-free: the site uses analytics and third-party assets.
- Do not promise universal browser support, animated-GIF preservation, a specific speed/savings figure, or no quality loss without a current test proving the claim.

The local branch was two commits behind `origin/main`; those remote commits concern WordPress plugin safety/format conversion and do not change the checked marketing claims.

## New draft-ready opportunities

### 1. MicroLaunch — recommended, free queue

- **Fit:** The official application accepts web tools/apps and free products. Fast Image Compression fits as an immediately usable browser tool.
- **Application:** https://tally.so/r/mYaR6N
- **Requirements observed:** name, account-matching email, country, product URL, codename, punchline under 10 words, approximately 70-word description, product type/category/offer/stage, approximate launch date, and a 128×128 JPG/PNG logo. A 680×340 hero image is optional and makes the product eligible for daily featuring.
- **Cost:** https://microlaunch.net/premium describes a free Regular/Basic launch with a 2–3+ month queue. The paid Pro route was shown as $49 standard pricing, with a temporary displayed discount; payment is unnecessary for this recommendation.
- **Review/scheduling:** The official form says applications are reviewed. The free route is automatically scheduled after the queue rather than launched immediately.
- **Activity:** The live platform showed an August 2026 leaderboard and recent launches during research.
- **Recommendation:** Use the free route when manual submission is authorized. Do not pay merely for a backlink or faster placement.
- **Draft:** `microlaunch.md`

### 2. DEV Community `#showdev` — recommended

- **Fit/rules:** https://dev.to/t/showdev explicitly covers showing projects and launching products. It requires posts to be community-driven rather than overly corporate or salesy.
- **Posting route:** https://dev.to/new (account required).
- **Cost:** No normal post fee was shown; paid membership and advertising are separate.
- **Approval:** User-published, subject to DEV moderation and its code of conduct: https://dev.to/code-of-conduct
- **Activity:** The official `#showdev` feed contained posts dated 2026-08-22 when checked.
- **Recommendation:** Publish only as a substantive builder post, disclose ownership, explain limitations, and ask for product/technical feedback rather than dropping a promotional link.
- **Draft:** `dev-community-showdev.md`

## SEO/content opportunity

Before sending reviewers from these channels, replace or substantiate the live footer claim **“The fastest online image compression tool. Compress images instantly without compromising quality.”** It conflicts with the conservative, evidence-based positioning used in these drafts. A safer replacement is: **“Compress and resize images locally in your browser. Selected image files are not uploaded to our server.”**

Also reconcile the README's “No data collection or tracking” wording with the site's disclosed analytics. These trust fixes remain higher priority than adding more near-duplicate keyword pages.
