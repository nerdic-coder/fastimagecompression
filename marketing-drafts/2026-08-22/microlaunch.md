## Optional long description

**Fast Image Compression** helps people prepare images for websites, WordPress, email, forms with file-size limits, and everyday sharing.

Selected image files are processed locally in the browser rather than uploaded to the application's server. Users can select one or multiple images, adjust quality and maximum dimensions, choose a browser-supported output format, or use a target-size workflow. The interface shows previews and file-size information before individual or batch downloads.

The tool is free to use and does not require registration. Output-format support depends on the browser, and users should review compressed results before replacing original files.
