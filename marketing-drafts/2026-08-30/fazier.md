## Channel decision

- **Platform:** Fazier — https://fazier.com/
- **Submission/pricing page:** https://fazier.com/submit
- **Official launch guide:** https://falakdigital.notion.site/Comprehensive-launch-guide-91568ec42751411d8050f1474121e7f8
- **Fit:** Reasonable secondary launch channel. Fazier is active, accepts free and freemium web products, and currently features browser utilities and image-related tools.
- **Status:** `hold`. The free Basic route requires a Fazier backlink badge on the product homepage or footer. Adding that badge would require an application-site change outside this marketing worktree's authorized scope. Paid Lite ($29 displayed), Premium ($49 displayed), and Super ($149 displayed) routes are not authorized.
- **Review/approval:** The Basic route says submissions are reviewed and listed within 30 days; homepage featuring is conditional. Paid routes advertise immediate/scheduled publication and additional promotion.
- **Account requirement:** The official guide says the submitter must create a Fazier account using Google or email.
- **Verified form fields:** Name, tagline, description, category, thumbnail, gallery, and pricing.
- **Duplicate check limitation:** Fazier exposes no public search route. The obvious `fastimagecompression` launch slug did not resolve to a listing, but this is not conclusive; recheck after signing in and before any submission.

## Recommended route

Do not add a third-party badge or buy placement merely to obtain a directory listing. If the user deliberately approves a homepage/footer backlink, use the free Basic route after reviewing the badge's visual and SEO impact. Otherwise keep Fazier on hold.

### Long description

Fast Image Compression is a free browser-based tool for preparing images for websites, WordPress, email, upload limits, and everyday sharing.

Selected image files are processed locally in the browser instead of being sent to the application's server. Choose one or several images, adjust quality and maximum dimensions, select an available output format, or use a target-size workflow. Preview the result and file-size change before downloading an individual image or batch output.

No registration is required to use the compressor. Output-format availability depends on browser encoding support, and users should review compressed results before replacing original files.
