## Product facts rechecked

The live homepage and repository README were checked today. Safe copy remains:

- Fast Image Compression is a free browser-based image compressor.
- Selected JPEG, PNG, and WebP image files are processed locally and are not uploaded to the application's server.
- The live site supports selecting multiple images and describes resizing, quality/format adjustment, previews, downloads, WordPress/email workflows, common target-size workflows, and offline use after the first load.
- The live site uses analytics. Keep privacy claims scoped to image-file processing; do not claim that the whole site has no tracking or data collection.
- The README remains stale in several places and should not be the sole source for current feature claims.
