About Fast Image Compression

Last updated: February 4, 2026

What this site is

Fast Image Compression is a simple, privacy-first tool that helps you reduce the file size of images (JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP) directly in your browser. The goal is practical: make images load faster on websites, take up less storage, and share quicker — without overthinking it.

Why it exists

Most people only need two things from an image compressor: (1) it should be easy, and (2) it should be trustworthy. Many online tools require uploads, accounts, or push users into expensive plans.

This project focuses on being:

  • In-browser: compression happens on your device.
  • Fast: optimized for quick batch processing.
  • Clear: straightforward UI and guides for real-world decisions.

Privacy approach

The compression workflow is designed so your images do not need to be uploaded to a server. For details about analytics/cookies and any third-party services, see our Privacy Policy.

Who runs it

Fast Image Compression is maintained by the creator of the site. If you have feedback, bug reports, or feature requests, the fastest way to reach us is via the Contact page.

Open-source

This project is developed publicly on GitHub. If you want to contribute or inspect the code, you can do so from the repository.
