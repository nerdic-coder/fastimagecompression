SEO keyword research for an image‑compression website (14 Sept 2025)
Methodology

I used WordStream’s Free Keyword Tool to search for “image compression” on 14 September 2025. The tool returned a list of 500 related keywords along with their average monthly search volume (from Google and Bing) and a competition level. The first 25 results were viewable without providing an email address. According to WordStream, all of the displayed keywords had a low competition score, meaning they are relatively easy to rank for.
