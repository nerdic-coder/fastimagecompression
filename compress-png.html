Transparency and format choices

If your PNG has transparency, choose WebP as the output format to keep the transparent background. JPEG does not support transparency and will flatten transparent areas.

Best PNG use cases

  • Logos, icons, and UI graphics.
  • Illustrations and screenshots with sharp edges.
  • Images that require transparent backgrounds.

Tips for smaller PNG files

  • Crop unused space before compressing.
  • Use WebP for photo-like PNGs to reduce size.
  • Keep dimensions close to the final display size.

PNG compression FAQ

Will PNG compression blur edges?

PNG is lossless, but output format changes can affect crisp edges. Use WebP if you need smaller files while keeping sharp details.

Can I keep transparency?

Yes, choose WebP output to keep transparency. JPEG does not support transparency.

Can I batch compress PNG files?

Yes. Upload multiple PNGs on the main tool and compress them in one run.
