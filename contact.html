Support email

For help, feedback, or bug reports, email us at: support@fastimagecompression.com

What to include

  • Which device and browser you’re using (e.g. iPhone Safari, Windows Chrome)
  • Image format(s) (JPEG/PNG/GIF/WebP)
  • What you expected vs what happened
  • Approximate file sizes (before/after) if relevant

Please do not email private images unless you’re comfortable sharing them. The tool is designed to work without uploads.

Business / partnerships

If you want to discuss partnerships or integrations, use the same email and include “Partnership” in the subject line.
